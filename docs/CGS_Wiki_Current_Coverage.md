## CGS 2.0 Brainstorm, page 2

Character identity, a dated assertion about that character, and a recorded action are independent records. For example, a single stable person can have several whereabouts intervals, participate in several events at once, and have a separate birthplace or origin. Person creation omits physical whereabouts. A dated event points back to the same person and can change the appropriate state. A participant or venue link alone leaves the person's whereabouts undetermined. An article's History view can read the resulting dates. ## Applying the findings

The [W1 revision plan](CGS_W1_Core_Worldbuilding.md#revision-batches-after-the-coverage-audit) turns these gaps into coding batches and defines the first batch's browser, API, storage and upgrade contract.

1. **Close W1 against the source checks.** Keep the stable identities and dated assertions. Add missing authored character and event data independent of execution, finish the scoped date and evidence contracts, and ship the persisted image map and linked pins. Correct NAV-07, the previously reproduced carry of a History date into an ordinary article click and subsequent fact form. The map locator and local layout must remain accessible under stable IDs when the editor arrives.
2. **Use one world model across W2 and W3.** Define a general-purpose rules and effects interface in W2. In W3 make the source types, links and their controls easy to find. A builder should be able to open a named catalog, find the correct template and edit a field or link in context. A reader should be able to browse a world home and category tree, follow search results and related articles, and reach an article with a summary, typed infobox, linked contents and sections, references, and map and history links. Give maps, events and calendars, relationship views, media and work planning dedicated routes. Preserve the source-defined distinctions and add distinct World Anvil and Fandom workflows in the original CGS treatment.
3. **Require full-stack evidence before making a parity claim.** For every source field and edge, show the named browser control, saved type or role, validation, linked read and applicable dated or scoped result. For each reference task, start at the world home and verify equivalent navigation, editing and reading after reload. Compare authored maps with map tools and actual encyclopedia articles with articles. Keep agreed later work and unverified gated workflows visible in the checklist. W5 finishes after the in-scope evidence passes.

Support for useful provisional representations, such as a map locator, a Group type choice or a free-text state, is evidence of the named limited workflow. It cannot close a different check for an editor, a typed relationship, or validated structured values. An outstanding field with no technical prerequisite remains an implementation gap even when it has a later milestone. The audit identified several such gaps and found no design reason to remove those source concepts.
